Duck Jalapeño Poppers

A smoky, creamy wild game appetizer wrapped in crispy bacon.

Recipe by Jason Dickson

Wild Game Appetizer • About 24 poppers

These Duck Jalapeño Poppers are packed with cream cheese, sharp cheddar, crispy bacon, and tender duck breast. They are perfect for hunting camp, game day, backyard cookouts, or family gatherings.

Easy Substitute

No duck on hand? Substitute venison, turkey, chicken, goose, sausage, pork, or ground beef.

Ingredients

  • 12 medium jalapeño peppers
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 4 oz sharp cheddar cheese, shredded
  • 1 lb duck breast, thinly sliced and chopped into bite-sized pieces
  • 4 strips bacon, cooked and crumbled
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • 12 slices bacon, for wrapping
  • Toothpicks

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 375°F and line a baking sheet with foil or parchment paper.
  2. Slice the jalapeños in half lengthwise. Remove the seeds and membranes, then rinse and pat dry.
  3. Combine the duck, cream cheese, cheddar, cooked bacon, garlic powder, smoked paprika, black pepper, and salt.
  4. Fill each jalapeño half with the mixture.
  5. Wrap each stuffed jalapeño with bacon and secure it with a toothpick.
  6. Place the poppers on the prepared baking sheet.
  7. Bake for 25–30 minutes, until the bacon is crisp and the jalapeños are tender.
  8. For extra-crisp bacon, broil for the final 2–3 minutes.
  9. Let the poppers cool slightly, then serve warm.

Serving Suggestions

Serve them as an appetizer, game-day snack, or side dish with grilled meat. They are especially good at hunting camp, tailgates, and backyard cookouts.

Tips

  • Wear gloves while handling jalapeños.
  • Leave some seeds in the peppers for extra heat.
  • Use a wire rack over the baking sheet for crisper bacon.
  • Cook duck or any substitute meat to a safe internal temperature.
