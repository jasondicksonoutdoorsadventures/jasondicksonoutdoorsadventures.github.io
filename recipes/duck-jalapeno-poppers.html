Duck Jalapeño Poppers

These Duck Jalapeño Poppers are a delicious wild game appetizer packed with cream cheese, cheddar cheese, crispy bacon, and tender duck breast. Wrapped in bacon and baked until golden brown, they're perfect for hunting camp, game day, backyard cookouts, or family gatherings.

Don't have duck? No problem! You can substitute almost any meat you like, making this recipe a favorite year-round.

Ingredients

  • 12 medium jalapeño peppers
  • 8 oz cream cheese, softened
  • 4 oz sharp cheddar cheese, shredded
  • 1 lb. duck breast, thinly sliced and chopped into bite-sized pieces
  • 4 strips bacon, cooked and crumbled
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • Salt to taste
  • 12 slices bacon (for wrapping)
  • Toothpicks

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
  2. Slice the jalapeños in half lengthwise and remove the seeds and membranes. Rinse and pat dry.
  3. Combine the duck, cream cheese, cheddar cheese, bacon, garlic powder, smoked paprika, black pepper, and salt.
  4. Fill each jalapeño half with the mixture.
  5. Wrap each stuffed jalapeño with a slice of bacon and secure with a toothpick.
  6. Place on a baking sheet lined with foil or parchment paper.
  7. Bake for 25–30 minutes or until the bacon is crispy and the jalapeños are tender.
  8. Optional: Broil for 2–3 minutes for extra crispy bacon.
  9. Allow to cool slightly before serving.

Serving Suggestions

Serve as an appetizer, game-day snack, or alongside your favorite grilled meats. They're always a hit at hunting camp, tailgates, and backyard cookouts.

Tips

  • 🦆 Don't have duck? Substitute venison, turkey, chicken, goose, sausage, pork, or even ground beef.
  • 🌶️ Wear gloves when handling jalapeños.
  • 🔥 Leave a few seeds in for extra heat.
  • 🥓 Broil during the last 2–3 minutes for extra crispy bacon.

Happy Cooking and Happy Hunting!
