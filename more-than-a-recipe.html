More Than a Recipe

Why the memories around the table are just as rewarding as the hunt.

By Jason Dickson

Published August 5, 2026 • Outdoor Cooking

One of the best parts of hunting isn't just the hunt itself. It's bringing family and friends together around the table afterward.

Whether it's venison, duck, goose, turkey, or fresh caught fish, preparing a meal from your own harvest is rewarding in a way that store bought food simply can't match. Every meal has a story behind it, from the early morning in the blind to the laughs shared while cooking in camp or at home.

This week's featured recipe is Duck Jalapeño Poppers. They're simple to make, packed with flavor, and always disappear quickly at camp, game day gatherings, or backyard cookouts.

Don't have duck? No problem. One of the great things about this recipe is that you can substitute almost any meat you have available. Venison, turkey, goose, chicken, pork, or even ground beef all work well.

The important thing isn't using a specific type of meat. It's sharing good food and creating memories with family and friends.
